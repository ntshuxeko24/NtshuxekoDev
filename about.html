About Me

Meet Ntshuxeko

A young developer passionate about technology, programming and creating useful digital experiences.

My name is Ntshuxeko Tshabalala . I am from Elim, Limpopo, South Africa.

I am passionate about technology, programming and web development. I enjoy learning how technology can be used to solve problems and help people and businesses.

I created NtshuxekoDev to help small businesses establish a professional presence online.

I believe that a good website should not only look modern, but should also be responsive, easy to use and useful to the people visiting it.

My goal is to continue developing my skills in programming and web development while creating websites that provide real value to businesses and their customers.

My Approach

Simple. Professional. Useful.

I focus on building websites that communicate clearly, work across different devices and give businesses a strong foundation for their online presence.
